A very short office comedy

The Cat Who Joined a Meeting

A cat walked across a laptop during a video meeting and accidentally typed, “mmmmmmmm.”

The manager nodded. “Excellent point. We should simplify the strategy.”

The cat pressed mute, knocked a pen off the desk, and went to sleep.

By Friday, it had been promoted to head of productivity.

No cats were overworked in the making of this story.
